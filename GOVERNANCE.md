# OpenAISF Governance (draft)

OpenAISF is intended to be a **neutral, open, vendor-independent** standard.
This document describes the governance model for the draft v0.1 period and
the intended model for v1.0.

## Principles

1. **Open participation.** Anyone may contribute via issues and pull requests.
2. **Vendor neutrality.** No single company controls the standard. The
   standard must be implementable and certifiable without favoring any
   vendor's product.
3. **Open licensing.** The spec is CC-BY-4.0; the reference CLI is Apache-2.0.
4. **Transparency.** Spec changes, crosswalk updates, and tier definitions are
   made in public.
5. **Stability.** Conformance is a contract. Breaking changes to control IDs
   or normative statements follow semantic versioning.

## Status during v0.1 (Draft / RFC)

During the Request-for-Comments period, the project is stewarded by the
initial author with the intention to transfer stewardship to a neutral
foundation (e.g., a Linux Foundation / OpenSSF-style home) once community
participation warrants it. All substantive decisions are made in public via
the RFC and issue tracker.

## Intended v1.0 governance

- A **Steering Group** of contributors from across labs, vendors, academia,
  and practitioners, with no single entity holding a controlling position.
- **Working Groups** per domain (e.g., D10 Tool/MCP, D4 Model Integrity,
  D9 Telemetry) to maintain controls and crosswalks.
- A **Conformance / Mark committee** that governs use of the OpenAISF
  conformance badge and accredits independent assessors (T3/T4).
- A documented **intellectual property / trademark policy** for the marks.

Comments on this governance model are explicitly in scope for RFC v0.1.

## Relationship to commercial products

Commercial products (including GRC tools, AI firewalls, and assessment
services) may implement, certify against, or build on OpenAISF. Such products
are **contributors to and consumers of** the standard, not its owners. The
standard's neutrality is independent of any implementation.
